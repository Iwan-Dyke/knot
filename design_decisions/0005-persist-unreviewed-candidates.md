# ADR 0005: Persist extracted assertions as unreviewed candidates

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-03
- Implementation: Implemented
- Documentation reviewed: 2026-11-07
- Related: [ADR 0004](0004-local-semantic-store-and-assistant-workflow.md)

## Context

Assistant extraction produces useful work that should survive the session without
requiring immediate human review. Saving an assertion must remain distinct from
accepting it for use as established knowledge.

## Decision

Persist extracted assertions immediately in the private local RDF dataset as
unreviewed candidates, with their source references and extraction provenance.
Do not promote them to accepted status merely because they were saved, passed
structural validation or were generated by a user-invoked assistant.

Keep review status explicit and inspectable. Candidate access must preserve that
status for human and AI consumers. ADR 0003 records the agreed default visibility
of both candidates and accepted assertions. The review interface and acceptance
operations remain separate design decisions. This decision does not
accept the named-graph representation proposed in ADR 0003.

## Alternatives and consequences

- Requiring approval before persistence keeps candidates out of the store, but
  interrupts extraction and risks losing work awaiting review.
- Immediate candidate persistence supports incremental review, at the cost of
  requiring consumers to distinguish unreviewed claims from accepted knowledge.
- Malformed RDF or assertions missing required provenance must fail validation
  explicitly; candidate status does not bypass structural checks.
