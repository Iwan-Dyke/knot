## Context

Describe the problem, constraints and evidence available when making the decision.
Use generic examples. Keep private source quotations and dataset outcomes outside
public design documentation.

## Decision

State the chosen approach and its scope. For a proposal, make that status explicit.
Name a design pattern only if it helps explain the responsibilities or tradeoff.
Keep command reference and current defaults in the relevant usage guide.

## Alternatives

Describe credible alternatives and why they were not selected. Explain why the
chosen approach fits this codebase rather than treating a pattern as a universal rule.

## Consequences

Record benefits, costs, risks, compatibility and any unresolved questions.

## Validation and implementation

Describe how the decision will be checked, what has actually shipped and what is
still pending. Link relevant guides, code or synthetic tests. Passing tests does
not imply acceptance of a proposed decision or independent validation of its claims.
