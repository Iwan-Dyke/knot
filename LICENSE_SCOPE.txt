scripts, build configuration, test code and original technical documentation,
subject to the exclusions below. The MIT licence text itself is unmodified.

-----------------

- Everything under data/, including notes, source documents, snapshots,

No permission to use or redistribute excluded personal, work-related or
research material is granted by the KNOT software licence. This exclusion
does not assert ownership of material belonging to another rights holder and
does not override any separately applicable licence or permission.

contains both software and dataset content: the applicable software licences
